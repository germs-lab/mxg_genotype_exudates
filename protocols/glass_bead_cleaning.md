# Glass bead cleaning protocol
**by Bolívar Aponte Rolón**

***Retroactively completed on 2026-05-20***

---

There are a lot of meathods out there. The general consensus is to soak in a strong acid or corrosive  to destroy DNA/RNA substrates, then rinse in a combination of DI water and use a dryying agent like ethanol, finally autoclave. See this [Reddit discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/labrats/comments/155jx5g/for_those_using_glass_beads_for_spreading_cells/) and this [other discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/labrats/comments/2xq8tk/how_do_you_wash_glass_beads_colirollers/).


## What I did:

### With new *unsterile* glass beads:

1. In 1L beaker, fill up to 100 - 150 mL line with glass beads.

2. Wash two or three times with just DI H2O.

3. Soak beads in 1 L of 0.1 N HCl (4 L DI H2O + 35 ml concentrated HCl) for 1-2 hours. *Alternatively 0.5% sodium hypochlorite (NaOCl) ( 10% Bleach solution) can be used.* 

4. Soak and rinse 3-4 times with DI H2O.

5. Soak in 95% EtOH for 10 minutes.

6. Repeat Step 3.

7. Wash beads with ethanol, drain and let dry in 1L beaker for 2 hours. *It can be left overnight in fume hood or biosafety cabinet, some folks dry overnight in 65° oven. In all depends on your sterile requirements.*

8. Aliquot beads into 50mL conical tubes. Fill up to 25-30 mL. 

9. Autoclave tube for 20 minutes at 121°C. Place cap but leave slightly open.

10. Let cool and close cap.

### With used beads:

Add these initial steps:

1. Using a 1-2 L beaker, fill up to 100 - 150 mL line with glass beads, rinse with tap water until supernatant is clear.

2. Wash two times with 1-2 L of 1%-2% Alconox DI H2O solution.

3. Continue with standard cleaning procedure. See above.
